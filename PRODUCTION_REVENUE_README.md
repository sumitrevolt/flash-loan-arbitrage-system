## 🎮 How It Works

### 1. **Opportunity Scanning**
- Continuously monitors prices across multiple DEXes
- Identifies arbitrage opportunities in real-time
- Calculates potential profits including all costs

### 2. **Risk Assessment**
- Evaluates each opportunity for risk level
- Considers gas costs, slippage, and market conditions
- Applies risk management rules

### 3. **Flash Loan Execution**
- Initiates Aave flash loan for optimal capital
- Executes arbitrage trades across DEXes
- Repays flash loan with profits

### 4. **Profit Distribution**
- Calculates net profits after all costs
- Tracks performance metrics
- Updates real-time monitoring
